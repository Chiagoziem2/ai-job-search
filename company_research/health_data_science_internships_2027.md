## Tier 1 — act within the next two weeks

### 1. HDR UK — Health Data Science Black Internship Programme (2027 cohort)
- **Source:** `hdruk.ac.uk` → Study and train → Health Data Science Black Internship Programme
- **Reported deadline:** Sunday **3 January 2027** *(one HDR UK page reported 15 January for a
  "pledge" deadline — the two disagree; verify)*
- **Reported open day:** **20 October 2026** — ten days from compilation. Reported information
  webinar: 19 November 2026.
- **Reported placement:** 8 weeks, Monday 28 June – Friday 20 August 2027
- **Reported eligibility:** restricted to **Black applicants**; must be UK-based for the
  recruitment process and the internship; **no visa sponsorship**. An older cycle's page
  described it as open to current UK students at any level, recent graduates, and
  international students registered in the UK, all requiring the right to work in the UK.
- **Past hosts:** Genomics England, Our Future Health, the Alan Turing Institute.
- **Why this is the single best-fit target on the list:** it is the only scheme found with a
  confirmed 2027 cycle, a deadline inside the planning horizon, hosts that are exactly the
  named target employers, and a placement window that matches a medical-school summer.
- **Two caveats that decide whether it is a target at all:**
  1. **Eligibility is ethnicity-restricted.** This is for the candidate to self-assess, not
     for anyone else to assume. The Melanin Medics research affiliation on the CV is suggestive
     but is not a statement of eligibility.
  2. **Right to work in the UK is unconfirmed** in the profile and the scheme reportedly does
     not sponsor. Settle this first — it gates everything.
- **Action now:** register interest / attend the 20 October open day. Registering is reversible
  and costs nothing; missing it is not recoverable.

### 2. Pharma R&D data-science summer internships — AstraZeneca, GSK, Roche
- **Sources:** each firm's own UK early-careers site (`careers.astrazeneca.com`, `jobs.gsk.com`,
  `careers.roche.com`)
- **Reported timing:** **these are the fastest-closing items found.** GSK's 2027 summer
  recruitment reportedly opened 8 September 2026, with one university listing showing an
  expiry of **9 October 2026 — i.e. possibly already past at compilation** — and further roles
  posted on a rolling basis from January 2027 through end of March. AstraZeneca's 2027 window
  reportedly opened early-to-mid September 2026 with rolling review and priority review closing
  through October 2026.
- **Caveat:** most of the guidance found was US-oriented and from third-party aggregator sites,
  not the companies. UK timing is genuinely unconfirmed. No UK 2027 data-science listing was
  found from any of the three.
- **Honest fit assessment: weak-to-moderate, and weaker than it looks.** These schemes
  overwhelmingly recruit from quantitative undergraduate degrees and industrial-placement
  years. An MBChB has no placement year, and the CV names no programming language. Worth an
  application as a lottery ticket; not worth displacing Tier 1 effort.
- **Action now:** check all three UK early-careers pages this week — if a window is open it may
  close within days.

## Tier 2 — right fit, cycle timing unconfirmed

### 3. NIHR INSIGHT (absorbing INSPIRE from 2027) — via Aston Medical School
- **Sources:** `nihr.ac.uk` → INSIGHT programme; **and Aston Medical School directly**
- **Status:** NIHR reports INSIGHT will incorporate the INSPIRE scheme for doctors and dentists
  from 2027 onwards. Funding runs in 3-year rounds; the most recent found was the 2026
  competition. No 2027 Aston-specific listing found.
- **Why this matters more than its ranking suggests:** **Aston Medical School holds £1.68m of
  NIHR funding specifically to develop intercalated degree and internship programmes.** This is
  an *internal* route at the candidate's own institution. It will likely never appear on a job
  board, and a medical student with three accepted conference abstracts and a health-data
  internship already behind them is close to the ideal applicant.
- **Action:** email Aston Medical School's research / undergraduate office. This is the highest
  expected-value action on the entire list relative to effort, and it is not a job application.

### 4. Health Data Insight (HDI) Internship Programme
- **Source:** `healthdatainsight.org.uk/hdi-internship-programme`
- **Reported:** open to undergraduates, postgraduates, and graduates up to 2 years out.
  Reported pay ~£2,066/month. Last cycle ran 2–3 months from July 2026; 2026 applications
  reported **closed**, with no 2027 call yet listed.
- **Fit:** good — eligibility explicitly includes undergraduates, and the work is health data.
- **Action:** watch the page; set a reminder for spring 2027.

### 5. HDR UK Midlands / PIONEER (Birmingham)
- **Source:** `hdrmidlands.org.uk`
- **Reported:** hosts undergraduate summer internship projects, run in person over the summer
  break. Pay reported at the real Living Wage (£13.45/hr) plus a travel and accommodation
  bursary up to £2,500 and £500 project costs — though that is the HDR UK–Wellcome BVS package,
  which the candidate **has already held** (see below).
- **Fit:** strong on geography (Birmingham, no relocation) and on subject.
- **Action:** check the page for a 2027 call, and email the hub directly regardless. A local hub
  with no advertised call will still answer a specific, credible enquiry.

### 6. University summer research bursaries — Nottingham first
- **Sources:** each university's undergraduate research opportunity page
- **Why Nottingham specifically:** there is an existing supervisor relationship on the TRExt
  project, a tool the candidate actually shipped (Lettuce Desktop), and a live project that
  continues. A returning intern is the cheapest, lowest-risk person a group can take on, and
  these placements are routinely arranged without ever being advertised.
- **Action:** email the TRExt / Lettuce supervisor. Like item 3, this bypasses the entire
  application-and-shortlist machinery.

## What the repo's `/scrape` could and could not contribute

- `linkedin-search` and `freehire-search` both returned **403 at the egress proxy** — blocked by
  this environment's network policy, not broken. They are untested against this market.
- Even unblocked, both are **structurally weak for this target category**. Academic schemes are
  advertised on funder and university pages with months-long lead times, under titles like
  *vacation scholarship*, *summer studentship* and *bursary* rather than *intern* — and
  `/scrape`'s 14-day recency window would actively hide them. `freehire` is tech-first by its
  own documentation and indexes software/ML engineering roles, not health-data schemes.
- The durable fix is committed in `.claude/skills/job-scraper/search-queries.md`: a **scheme
  watchlist as the primary mechanism**, with keyword scraping demoted to a supplement that
  catches the commercial postings (pharma, NHS, health-tech) which genuinely do appear as ads.
